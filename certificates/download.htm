The 20 Most Affordable Online Certificates in Digital Marketing

Tweet This Share This Share This Pin This Subscribe
Digital campaigns are completely omnipresent, incredibly personalized, and, at their best, highly engaging. Digital marketing goes way beyond banners and pop-ups, to encompass all audience engagement and customer relationships across all digital channels, from search engines and websites, to social media and mobile apps. And for every digital marketing campaign or promotion, there’s a major investment of behind-the-scenes digital marketing research and analysis to make sure the right message is reaching the right audience. Welcome to the complex, creative, and cutting-edge world of Digital Marketing. If you’re new to the field, a Digital Marketing Certification is a great way to launch your career in this stimulating and fast-evolving field. If you’re an experienced professional, you know that Digital Marketing Certifications can develop a specialization and showcase your skill set for employers. Certifications are targeted, high-impact credentials, and are often available at a surprisingly low cost. Many of the most affordable Digital Marketing Certifications are offered in an online format, making them also a convenient option for busy professionals. An online Digital Marketing Certification differs significantly from an associate’s, bachelor’s or master’s degree. An online associate’s degree in marketing is a two-year program (when taken full-time) to help new graduates get their foot in the door, usually while working towards a bachelor’s degree. An online bachelor’s degree in marketing is a four-year program generally designed for recent college graduates preparing for entry-level positions, and an online master’s degree is a two-year program generally aimed at mid-career professionals looking to advance their skills to gain a managerial or executive position. An online Digital Marketing Certification, on the other hand, is an affordable way to efficiently acquire specific skill sets, either as a way to launch a digital marketing career or to bolster an existing one. You’ll gain expertise specific to the digital domain, allowing you to become specialized in an efficient and affordable format. In fact, may students enrolled in an online degree in marketing at the associate’s, bachelor’s or master’s level choose to pursue a digital marketing certification at the same time as a way to specialize their skills and to demonstrate specific expertise to employers.
